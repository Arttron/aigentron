Aigentron v0.1.91

For people who are not programmers: a project's chat now hears only about that project, and adding a Telegram channel has a "Find my id" helper.

New
- Find my id (Settings → Channels → add or edit a Telegram channel). Once the bot token is entered, press "Wait for my message", write anything to your bot from your own account, and the form shows what you wrote and your id, then asks "Add this id to the allowed chats?". Yes adds it to the list (saved with the channel). The test message is read and confirmed, so it never becomes a task. For a channel that is already running, the form watches the waiting chats instead (two programs cannot read one bot).

Fixed
- A chat that belongs to a project (the project's notification channel and chat) still received the approvals and questions of every task. They now go to the task's project chat only, and a chat owned by a project hears nothing about other projects' tasks. A project without its own chat skips the chats other projects own. If the chat of a project is not in the channel's allowed list, nothing is sent to it.

Update note: no database changes.
