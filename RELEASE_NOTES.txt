Aigentron v0.1.51

For people who are not programmers: agents can now ask you questions with answer buttons, you can talk to them about a photo or a video frame, and agents know what time it is.

Questions with buttons
- An agent that needs a decision asks you right away, with answer buttons, instead of guessing or stopping. The card appears at the bottom right of the dashboard on every page; in Telegram it arrives as a message with buttons.
- Pick one, or tick several (☐/☑ in Telegram with a "✅ Done" button; checkboxes in the dashboard). You can also just reply with text — a number picks that option, "1, 3" picks several.
- The first answer wins and every place updates at once. If nobody answers within an hour, the agent chooses a safe default itself or reports that it is blocked.
- A question can show a picture, for example a video frame: "is this the crack?".
- The English starter pack (tutor and quizmaster) uses it for drills. Packs you already installed keep their old agents — reinstall the pack to get it.

Talking about photos and video
- Click a photo or video in a task to open a viewer: pause on a moment (or take the photo as it is), draw a red rectangle round what you mean, type your question. The marked picture goes to the agent with the file name and the time.
- Agents can ask for video frames at the moments the speaker says "look here" / "смотри, вот тут", every N seconds, or at exact times; the speech transcript now has times.

Smaller changes
- Agents have a clock: the new current_time tool gives the date, day of the week and time (in the server's timezone, or the one asked for).
- Telegram command menu: agents show up in the "/" autocomplete reliably. A failed publish used to be silent and never retried, so agents that already existed could be missing from the menu.
- "Default lead agent" moved to the Agents page.

Update note: this release adds a small table and one column for the questions; they are created automatically when the server starts.
