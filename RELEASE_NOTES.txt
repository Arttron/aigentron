Aigentron v0.1.41

For people who are not programmers: the terminal setup menu is easier to get out of.

Setup wizard (`aigentron`)
- Every menu now ends with "← Back" (one level up) or "Exit" (where there is nothing above), so you are never stuck after choosing something like "Delete a provider".
- Esc does the same from anywhere: in a menu, while typing a value, or at a "really do it?" question. Nothing is saved when you cancel; a cancelled delete deletes nothing.
