Aigentron v0.1.84

For people who are not programmers: a better photo viewer, a full-screen conversation, fewer copies of the same file, and a fix for attaching files on a phone.

New
- Photo viewer: arrow keys, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End and Esc; a click on the left or right edge of the picture steps back or forward; swipe on a phone; the whole screen on a phone; the next pictures are loaded in advance.
- A task's conversation can fill the screen (the "Full screen" button in its card; Esc exits). A half-typed message and the scroll position are kept.
- The task menu on a phone closes when you press outside it, press Esc or choose an action.
- Fewer copies of files. A lead shows what its subtasks produced by reference - a picture in a message as ![caption](task:ID/file.png), another file as a link - instead of copying it into its own chat; the same works through show_images and reaches Telegram. show_images links workspace and library files instead of copying them, and showing the same picture twice no longer makes a second copy. Agents are told to report their own result instead of quoting the subtasks' answers.

Fixed
- Attaching files to a new task on a phone: the size limit now follows the file kind (photos and documents up to 10 MB like the server, video up to 95 MB) and is checked after a photo is shrunk and before the task exists; a file that cannot be read or uploaded is named with the reason; a task that did not get its files is removed and your text and files stay in the form for another try (no duplicate tasks); the button shows the upload progress.
- Safer defaults for small servers: the low-priority wrapper is tried out before use, and the memory guard from 0.1.83 is kept as it is.

Update note: no database changes.
