Aigentron v0.2.4

For people who are not programmers: the program on your computer can now keep running in the background and start by itself when you sign in.

Added
- "aigentron service install" keeps the local agent running in the background and starts it at sign-in (macOS and Linux); "aigentron service status" shows whether it runs, "aigentron service uninstall" turns it off. Nothing is started in the background unless you ask for it.
