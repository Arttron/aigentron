Aigentron v0.1.44

For people who are not programmers: you can now pick a provider's model from a list.

Setup wizard (`aigentron providers` → Edit a provider)
- The model question is a menu: keep the current model, choose from the models the provider offers (for example the list from your Ollama or an API-key provider), or type a name.
- Where a provider cannot list its models (a Claude subscription login), the wizard says so and offers the current Claude models instead.
