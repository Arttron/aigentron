Aigentron v0.1.102

For people who are not programmers: an agent's answer no longer disappears after a page reload.

Fixed
- An agent often wrote its answer (a checklist, a list, an explanation) as a message in the middle of its work and then finished with a short "ready, see above". Those messages were shown live but were not stored, so after a reload, in a chat or when the task was opened later only "see above" remained. Now the agent's text messages are stored like its final answer (tool calls and thinking are still kept only in debug mode). Messages of tasks that have already run cannot be recovered.
- The agents' charter now says the final message must contain the deliverable itself and never refer to "the answer above".

Update note: no database changes.
