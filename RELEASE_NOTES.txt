Aigentron v0.1.71

For people who are not programmers: a safer "Mark as done" for tasks that have subtasks.

New
- When you accept a task that still has unfinished subtasks, the dashboard now asks what to do with them: "Cancel them and mark done", "Mark done, leave them" or "Back". Accepting a task never accepts its subtasks by itself.
- In Telegram, /done now tells you how many subtasks are still open.

Update note: no database changes.
