Aigentron v0.1.35

For people who are not programmers: the setup wizard in the terminal is much nicer to use.

Setup wizard (node infra/setup-wizard.mjs)
- Menus you move through with the arrow keys (or a number) and Enter; Yes/No switches you toggle with ← → or y / n.
- A main menu: guided setup, or jump straight to Providers, Channels, Agents, Repository or Advanced settings, and come back to it.
- Colours: steps, successes, errors and warnings are easy to tell apart. Set NO_COLOR=1 to turn them off.
- Fixed: the wizard no longer hangs after the last step — it finishes and returns you to the shell.
- Without a terminal (piped input, scripts) it still works with plain typed answers.
