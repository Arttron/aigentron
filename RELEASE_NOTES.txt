Aigentron v0.1.69

For people who are not programmers: fewer stuck tasks and a simple way to resume them.

What is new
- ▶ Continue. A task that stopped (stalled, blocked or failed) now has a "Continue" button on its page; in Telegram the same is `/continue` (optionally with a note: `/continue use the second approach`). The agent resumes the same session and carries on.
- Used-up limits. When a provider's plan, balance or 5-hour / weekly limit runs out, the task fails over to the agent's fallback provider, and for the next 30 minutes new runs start on the fallback straight away. A task that still stops explains what to do (add a fallback provider or wait for the reset).
- The task list remembers the project you filtered by when you come back to it.

Fixes
- An approval or question card no longer hangs after you decided it in Telegram or another tab while the page was disconnected: the page re-reads the pending list on reconnect and every 30 seconds.

Update note: no database changes.
