Aigentron v0.1.97

For people who are not programmers: the update card now shows that an update is running, in any tab, and the update block is back at the top of Settings → General.

Fixed
- While an update is running (started from this page, another tab or the console), Settings → General → Version & updates says so: "Updating to X — started N min ago", with what the installer is doing right now and a warning with the commands to look at it if nothing has been written to the update log for five minutes. Meanwhile "an update is available", the Update button and Check now are hidden, and pressing Update a second time shows the running update instead of just an error.
- The Version & updates card is the first card of the General tab again (it had slipped down in the settings regrouping).

Update note: no database changes.
