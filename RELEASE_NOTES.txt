Aigentron v0.2.11

For people who are not programmers: the program on your computer can now do work for your agents, and attachments can be deleted.

New
- Your agents can work on your computer, the same way the browser extension lets them work in the browser. They can look through a project folder, search it, change files, create new ones and run commands. Before the first change in a folder the dashboard asks you once (a card); it is off until you allow it on that computer with `aigentron writes on`.
- A Stop button (dashboard: Settings > Local agent, or `aigentron stop`) cuts running commands at once and keeps every agent out until you allow them again.
- Only one task works in a folder at a time, and secret files (.env, keys) and the .git folder are never opened. Note: commands are not locked into the folder yet.
- The agent can propose a commit and notices appear in the project's chat.
- A ready-made agent "local-machine" (executor on your own computer) in the catalog.
- Attachments can be deleted: a cross on each file in the task's list. A file that only points to another place is unlinked, the original stays.

Fixed
- A picture you attach was shown twice when the agent opened it: the program saved its own copy. Not any more.

Aigentron v0.2.10

For people who are not programmers: the program on your computer now looks after your unsaved work by itself.

New
- Safety copies of your unsaved work. While the program is running, it keeps a copy of your changes after you stop touching them for 2 minutes (the last 20 are kept). Your files, your branch and your staging area are never touched, and secret files (.env, keys) are never copied.
- `aigentron snapshots <repository>` lists the copies; `aigentron restore <repository> <copy>` puts them back (it will not overwrite fresh changes unless you add --force, and then it saves them first).
- The program fetches the latest changes from your remote every 5 minutes, so the dashboard shows how far behind you are. It never changes your files for this.
- Optional: "fast-forward" (off by default) brings a clean branch up to date with its remote when you have no changes of your own on it.
- The program waits and does nothing while you are in the middle of a merge or rebase, while git is busy, or while you are typing. All of this can be tuned or switched off in the rules, and every automatic action appears in the journal (`aigentron log on`).
