Aigentron v0.1.61

For people who are not programmers: Telegram and the chat channels were reworked to survive restarts and long messages, and known security advisories in the libraries were fixed.

Telegram and channels
- Long answers are split into several messages (at paragraph or line boundaries) instead of being cut at Telegram's 4096-character limit.
- Buttons on approval and question messages keep working after a restart of the server; attachments are not sent a second time after a restart; the bot remembers where it stopped reading updates, so restarting or editing a channel no longer replays old messages (no duplicate tasks).
- Questions and approvals of a subtask go straight to the chat of the task that created it. If nobody answers in the chat, an escalation check now delivers them to the default chat (every 30 seconds), also when the dashboard was in focus at that moment and was closed later.
- A scheduled run no longer takes over a chat where you are talking to another task right now.
- Telegram requests have time limits, and a "too many requests" answer is retried automatically.

Security
- Libraries with published vulnerabilities were updated or pinned to patched versions (the audit of runtime dependencies went from 1 critical and 17 high findings to 0 and 3; the remaining ones are in Prisma's own tooling, not in the running server).
- The built-in research tool now needs a per-run key (agents get it automatically; it expires after 3 hours) and refuses more kinds of internal addresses, including tricks with IPv6 and DNS.
- Secret settings files are read through a short cache (2 seconds) and written atomically.
- Release archives are published together with a SHA256SUMS file, which the installer verifies.

Other
- The Stats page says that totals before 0.1.60 are overstated.
- Lighter database reads for task actions; one PDF reading budget (45 s) instead of two.

Update note: no database changes.
