Aigentron v0.2.5

For people who are not programmers: a folder on your computer can now be shared with a project even when the project has no repository address, and the background service reports its state more clearly.

Changed
- A project that has a repository address still needs the shared folder to be that repository. A project without an address no longer blocks sharing: your "Share" is the consent, and the folder's name does not matter. (Server-side change: update the server.)
- "aigentron service status" shows one clear line (state and process number) instead of repeated ones.
