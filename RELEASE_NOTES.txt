Aigentron v0.1.101

For people who are not programmers: the branches that agents create now have readable names.

Changed
- A task's branch is named after its title instead of its id: `agent/fix-login-button-6yvhs1` (a few meaningful words, Cyrillic transliterated, at most 28 characters, plus the last 6 characters of the task id so names never clash). A task whose title gives nothing usable keeps the old `agent/task-<id>` form; branches that already exist are not renamed.
- Cleanup of old branches (Settings → maintenance, the admin's cleanup) recognises both forms; a branch that belongs to no task is never listed or deleted.
- In No-approvals mode merging the agent's own task branches in its worktree stays free for both forms of names.

Update note: no database changes.
