Aigentron v0.1.50

For people who are not programmers: agents can now see photos and watch videos, and you can take pictures right from the page.

Camera and video
- New "📷 Camera" in the attach menu (new task, message box) and on the Resources page. Over HTTPS it opens in the page: take several photos or record a clip, check them, attach. Without HTTPS your phone's own camera opens. Big photos are shrunk and their hidden data (such as location) is removed.
- Videos up to 300 MB can be attached to tasks and kept in the Resources library, and they play in the dashboard (seeking works on phones too).
- Telegram: videos, round video messages and GIFs now arrive as attachments. (Telegram bots can download only up to 20 MB; for bigger files the bot asks you to upload them in the dashboard.)
- While an agent works, Telegram chats show "typing…", "recording a voice message…" or "sending a photo/video/document…".

How agents watch a video
- The sound is turned into text with the time of each phrase, and frames are cut out of the picture. Agents can ask for a frame every N seconds, at exact times, or at the moments the speaker says "look here" / "смотри, вот тут" (or any words you choose).
- Settings → Vision: choose which model describes photos and video frames (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, or a local Ollama vision model), with a Test button. Settings → Voice is used for the sound (a Whisper model gives the timestamps).
- ffmpeg is now installed by the installer and is part of the Docker images.

Smaller changes
- "Default lead agent" moved from Settings to the Agents page and saves as soon as you pick.
- The terminal (aigentron / aigentron admin) reuses its sign-in, shows as one device in the list, and `aigentron logout` ends the session on the server.

Update note: if ffmpeg could not be installed automatically, run `aigentron doctor`; video features show a clear message until it is available.
