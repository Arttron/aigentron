Aigentron v0.1.93

For people who are not programmers: a project's chat gets its approvals at once, a subtask's answer reads as that agent's message, long answers fold, and picking tasks in the list is simpler.

New
- Approvals and questions of a project that has its own chat go to that chat immediately (before: after about 10 seconds, and not at all while the dashboard was open). A project without a chat, the default one too, is announced only in a free chat that no project owns. The project's chat must be among the channel's allowed chats.
- In the conversation of a lead task, the report of each subtask now shows as a message from that subtask's agent (for example "designer>") instead of "you>", without the instructions that were sent to the lead. Older reports keep their old look.
- Long messages of agents with a name (subtask reports, discussion turns) fold to ten lines with "Show more".
- Picking tasks in the list has no mode any more: every row has a checkbox on the left, the bar above the list is always there and its buttons (Mark as done, Cancel, Delete) switch on once something is ticked, so the page no longer jumps. Shift-click ticks a range.
- Checkboxes and radio buttons everywhere are drawn in the style of the interface (both themes).

Update note: no database changes. A new type of conversation entry is stored (a subtask's report); nothing needs to be done.
