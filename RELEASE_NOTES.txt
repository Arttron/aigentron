Aigentron v0.1.122

For people who are not programmers: two fixes to everyday chat use.

Fixed
- In the message box, selecting text and pressing Backspace or Delete (or typing over the selection) did nothing — "select all, Backspace" could not clear the box. It works again in every message box (task follow-up, new task, admin chat).
- In a task's conversation, an agent that only answered a question was recorded as "blocked" ("I need your answers") after every reply, so the task looked failed. A reply that is just conversation is now recorded as done; "blocked" stays for work that really cannot go on without your decision. (This relies on the agent following the instruction, so a model may still occasionally say "blocked".)
